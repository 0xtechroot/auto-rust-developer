## 🎯 Why This Schedule is Realistic

### **4-Hour Intervals:**
✅ Mimics real developer work sessions
✅ Natural breaks for meetings, lunch, breaks
✅ Not too frequent (avoids bot detection)
✅ Not too sparse (shows consistent activity)
✅ Sustainable long-term pattern

### **1-3 Commits Per Run:**
✅ Realistic batch work (not single-line changes)
✅ Varies naturally (sometimes small fixes, sometimes features)
✅ Looks like actual development sessions
✅ Includes delays between commits (30-120 seconds)
